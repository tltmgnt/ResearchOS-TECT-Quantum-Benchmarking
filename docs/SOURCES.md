# Primary sources and engagement context

Reviewed November 16, 2026. Research alignment does not establish endorsement, eligibility, funding or acceptance.

- [DARPA Quantum Benchmarking Initiative](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/quantum-benchmarking-initiative): evaluation of pathways to utility-scale quantum computing, including independent verification and validation.
- [DARPA QBI 2026, DARPA-PA-26-02](https://www.darpa.mil/work-with-us/opportunities/darpa-pa-26-02): fault-tolerant system approaches and workflows containing quantum computation.
- [SAM.gov Stage A QBIT, DARPA-PA-26-02-02](https://sam.gov/opp/743a55e6e10847b0b8104348a887e4ae/view): topic-specific scope and attachments. Check the current notice and amendments for submission instructions; do not rely on a cached program summary or this repository for deadlines.

The opportunity excerpt provided by the project owner states that proposals must respond to a specific QBIT rather than directly to the parent PA. Confirm that requirement against the applicable live notice before submission.

The simulator uses explicitly documented elementary gate mathematics. Resource factors and example timings are illustrative owner-supplied fixtures, not DARPA requirements or calibrated architecture estimates. No numerical performance or utility claims are inferred from the program pages.
