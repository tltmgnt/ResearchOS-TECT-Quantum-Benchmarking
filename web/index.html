METHOD & RESEARCH CONTEXT

Make every boundary explicit.

Exact, bounded simulation

An all-zero initial state, H/X/Z/CX operations, and ideal computational-basis probabilities. Seeded MT19937 sampling follows Python’s integer-seed convention. No noise, decoding, error correction or hardware execution is modeled.

1–12 qubits · Up to 10,000 gates · 20 million amplitude operations · Up to 1 million shots

Declared resource assumptions

Physical qubits, sequential-shot runtime and a conditional failure union bound are simple accounting calculations. Their validity depends on your workload correspondence and assumptions.

The browser rejects derived integers beyond safe precision. Use the Python CLI for larger accounting workloads.

QBI research alignment

This independent workspace supports reproducible benchmarking and evidence-linked evaluation. It is not a QBI performer, a submission, or a government validation service. Check the applicable QBIT and amendments for any engagement or submission requirements.
